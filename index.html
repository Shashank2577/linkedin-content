How tracking works

1. Post on LinkedIn (native scheduler or manual) → drop the source link in the first comment manually (schedulers can't do it).
2. Open the post's card here → paste the LinkedIn URL into Mark as posted → copy the generated line and send it to Claude.
3. Claude verifies the post through your logged-in browser, pulls impressions / reactions / comments / reposts, updates this page, commits and pushes. Checks run when you ask — suggested cadence: 2h / 24h / 72h after posting.
4. No unattended scraping: LinkedIn blocks it. We read your analytics through your browser session only.
