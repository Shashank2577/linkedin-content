## The 5 attention strategies — definitions + guardrails

1. **Newsjacking** — ride a breaking story. Window: 24–48h. After that you're
   not jacking news, you're doing analysis, and analysis needs an angle the
   day-1 posters missed. Never restate the news; add the thing only you can add.
2. **Namejacking** — attach to a famous person's moment. The execution surface
   is THEIR comment section (first ~20 comments or you're invisible), not your
   post. In your own posts, reference their work by name. Never tag them
   hoping for a reshare — it reads thirsty and LinkedIn can suppress it.
3. **Brandjacking** — intercept a big brand's news cycle. When Anthropic or
   OpenAI is the story, the conversation already exists; your job is to
   redirect it to your lane (production reliability), not to summarize it.
4. **Hot take** — a position that could lose you followers, stated plainly.
   Guardrail: it must be a position you'd defend in a client meeting. Your
   brand is "honest limits beat hype" — rage bait is off-brand, a sharp
   uncomfortable truth is on-brand.
5. **Trend-riding** — connect your post to the meta-narrative running across
   the week. Name the trend out loud. Single posts ride news; arcs ride trends.

**This week's meta-trend: "agents escaped the demo."** NYT visa story (Oct 9)
and the Wikimedia incident (Oct 7) are the SAME story. Tuesday is episode 1,
Sunday is episode 2 — cross-reference them and you own the arc, not just two
disconnected reactions.
