## Post body (copy from here)

The most useful line in Replit's model news isn't the switch. It's that Claude is still selectable.

Replit CEO Amjad Masad said onstage at TechCrunch Disrupt that OpenAI is now the preferred partner for powering Replit Agent. His reasons were cost, partnership and performance, and he thinks ChatGPT is now better at building apps from scratch. Afterwards he clarified that customers can still use Anthropic models. ChatGPT is just the default. TechCrunch notes most customers keep the default, so that one setting could move a lot of spend.

Parts of the coverage disagree. An unnamed insider says the Anthropic partnership is still in place, and a Replit blog post from late September had Claude models running in the Agent's production environment. I wouldn't pick a side from outside.

What I'd take from it either way: if changing your default model is a settings change, you built it right. If it's a migration project, your vendor chose for you.

Masad also said agents need orchestration, error recovery, sandboxing, and identity and authorization controls before they can be deployed safely. I'd put that list on the wall.
