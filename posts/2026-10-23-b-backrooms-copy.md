## Post body (copy from here)

The copy of their game still had their family videos inside.

Steelkrill Studio made The Backrooms 1998, a found-footage horror game released in 2025. Then a near-identical copy appeared for sale under a different publisher. The developer believes the copier used a decompiler. The giveaway: the copy still contained their personal and family videos.

According to 404 Media's reporting, the publisher, Cool Devs, appears to have been banned. The game later resurfaced under other names, including The Bad Parents from TrueMotion Interactive.

I'm telling this one because it's the unglamorous side of cheap decompilation. Preservation projects get the headlines. Copy-and-resell gets the same tools, and it doesn't clean up after itself.

For anyone shipping a game or an app: assume your binary will be read. Keep personal data and secrets out of what you ship, because they travel with the copy.
