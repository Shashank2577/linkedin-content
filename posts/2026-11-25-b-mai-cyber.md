## Post body (copy from here)

92.3% on CyberGym-E2E at under 2% of GPT-6 Astra's cost per task.

That's Microsoft's pitch for MAI Cyber 2 Flash.

The pricing is the real story. It's $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.01 cached and $0.50 output. GPT-6 Astra on Azure OpenAI is $10, $1 and $50. That's a 100x gap on input, before you count the smaller model's lower token volume.

Microsoft says the model hits 92.3% on CyberGym-E2E against Astra's 91.5%, with a 1M-token context window that's four times its previous cyber model. It's already doing about 40% of MDASH scans.

The footnote deserves attention. Microsoft says 92 of the 905 CyberGym-E2E tasks come from the same training data, so the reported result covers the remaining 813. That's more disclosure than most labs give. It still isn't an independent run.

For security teams, the useful case is volume. Triage every dependency alert, every suspicious PR, every config drift. Keep the expensive model for the incident that actually pages someone.

At this price, the bottleneck stops being inference cost and becomes how many alerts your team can review.
