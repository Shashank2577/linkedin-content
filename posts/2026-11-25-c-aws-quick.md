## Post body (copy from here)

The most useful agent feature I saw this week was a tab that keeps working after you close it.

AWS Quick now runs flows and agents in the background, then puts the results in a Mission Control queue. That's a small change with a big effect.

The rest of the release is about turning one person's process into a team process. You describe a workflow in chat, and Quick builds a flow that orchestrates agents, skills, tools and data. Fixed calculations and checks sit alongside the agent steps, so the numbers are reproducible. Flows can run longer, handle larger data volumes, and run from web, desktop or mobile.

The part I'd test first is the deterministic step. "Agents draft and analyze, fixed steps calculate and check" is the right split for finance work. It's also the split most agent builders skip. That's where trust gets built.

There's a real customer number: Pomelo says nearly four times as many people now work with data each month, and one use case saves 80 hours a month. That's a vendor-selected quote, not a controlled study. I'd still take the meeting.

Quick is also adding Meta ads and Amplitude connectors through MCP, group-level permissions, prompt guardrails, and Japanese, German, Chinese and Vietnamese.

The catch is that this is AWS's workspace. The value only shows up if your data and identity already live there. Otherwise you're buying a second place to work. I've seen that movie.
