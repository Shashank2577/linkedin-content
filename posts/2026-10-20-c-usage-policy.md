## Post body (copy from here)

Here's the checklist I'd run on Anthropic's updated usage policy before it takes effect on November 12. Steal it.

I read the October 8 post looking for new agent rules. There aren't any, beyond physical-action controls. The rest is consolidation and clarification. It's still worth reading like a dependency diff.

1. Physical actions. If Claude is connected to hardware that can cause injury, a qualified operator must be able to observe it and stop it, and the equipment must hold a safe state if Claude disconnects.

2. Surveillance. Tracking people without consent is out, live or from past data. Claude can't decide or recommend who to investigate, arrest or charge.

3. Deceptive activity. One new section covers hiding who is behind a message and fake-account amplification. It pulls together rules that used to sit in several places.

4. High-risk use cases. Requirements haven't changed: a qualified human in the loop and notice to affected people that AI was used. The section now spells out which recommendations count.

5. Regions. Enforcement covers entities incorporated, headquartered or majority-controlled from unsupported regions. Check your client's corporate structure, not just where the users sit.

Anthropic says enforcement is unchanged for the clarified items. Run this against what's already in production, not what you're planning.
