## Post body (copy from here)

A longer vulnerability report is not how you get safer.

Anthropic's new Cyber Mission has two starting points: a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program for the people who protect power, water and transport systems, and an OSS Scanner, an opt-in service that scans open-source projects for free and sends reports with a proof of concept, an explanation and a suggested fix.

Look at what they admit. The reports are model-generated and go out without human review, so some will have wrong severity ratings. They expect a true-positive rate above 90%. And the page says verifying, prioritizing and fixing findings is still hard: in Project Glasswing, months often passed between discovery and fix.

Finding got cheap. Fixing didn't.

If you're adopting AI-driven scanning, the number I'd track isn't findings per week. It's median days from report to merged fix, and how many reports a human had to throw away.

A scanner that finds 200 things and a team that fixes 5 has a backlog, not security.
