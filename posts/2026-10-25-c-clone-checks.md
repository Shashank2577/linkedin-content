## Post body (copy from here)

If you're about to trust a free clone of Word or Photoshop with a client file, run these five checks first.

Reports on Hacker News about the new Office-style apps ran from "works well" to "barely functions," so it's worth testing before you rely on them. These are my checks, not results. I haven't run the apps.

1. Round-trip a real file. Open a document the original app made, save it, and compare. Did anything change that you didn't touch?

2. Read the license and the repo. The storytold apps I looked at are Apache-2.0. Know what that covers and what it doesn't.

3. Look at who maintains it. The org's own stats show 116 releases and over 870 human contributors, with most changes disclosing an agent. Decide if you're comfortable with that for your use.

4. Test the painful edges: large files, undo history, odd characters, export and print.

5. Keep the original app for the final export until you've compared the output side by side.

A free tool that fails on the one file that matters isn't free.
