## Post body (copy from here)

Anthropic shipped 17 manufacturing skills and four connectors. The factory floor is now an agent surface.

Claude for Manufacturing is a plugin for Team and Enterprise plans. The skills cover quoting a new job, investigating a failed inspection, diagnosing a breakdown, sourcing from another supplier, comparing designs in Autodesk Fusion, and reporting on production. The connectors are Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, and Oracle NetSuite.

The workflow is the point. An estimator can have Claude read a customer request, compare costs on similar jobs, and draft the quote. A quality engineer can have it document a failure, check past reports for the same problem, rank likely causes, and draft the corrective action report. A technician can feed it sensor exports, repair notes, photos, and the manual, then get a ranked list of causes to check.

Anthropic says every skill is built to end with a person's review, and Claude can link figures and claims back to the underlying data. Infor is also releasing its own plugin that can make changes in Infor, with Claude's access following the buyer's permissions.

Caveat: it doesn't operate equipment. Anthropic is still developing the Model Hardware Standard for that.

The bigger signal: vertical agents are moving from demos to packaged skills with connectors and approval gates. That's what enterprise adoption actually looks like.
