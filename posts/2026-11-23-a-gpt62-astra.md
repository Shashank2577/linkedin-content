## Post body (copy from here)

5.5 points.

That's the gap GPT-6.2 Astra opened over Claude Fable 5.2 on Agents' Last Exam, at 5% of the cost per task.

OpenAI published it on November 17. Astra also lands 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra on the same eval while using less than a third of the cost. On Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 it adds 9 percentage points at the same sub-one-third cost.

The part I'd read twice is the footnote. Fable 5.2's leaderboard entry leaves out nine tasks where the model fell back to a different checkpoint. OpenAI flags the exclusion, so the headline gap isn't a clean apples-to-apples run.

Pricing is $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached, $40 output. That's 20% below GPT-6 Astra, with a 60% cache discount. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol pricing and lands within 0.5 points of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost.

So the routing decision is straightforward. Sol for routine agent loops. Astra for the tasks that keep failing. And measure cost per accepted task, because Astra also used 62% fewer tokens than GPT-6.1 on OpenAI's agent evals.
