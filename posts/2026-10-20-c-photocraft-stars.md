## Post body (copy from here)

68,700 stars. That's what a repo called PhotoCraft has collected in 20 days.

It was created on September 30 and is Apache-2.0. Press coverage and Hacker News describe it as a Photoshop-style app, one of a family of free apps from a group called storytold under the ArtCraft banner. A week later came WordCraft and GridCraft, Word-style and Excel-style apps, created October 7 and already at about 14,800 and 5,900 stars.

The number I'd look at is in the repo that tracks them. storytold-stats counts 9,741 released changes across the org repos it covers. Of those, 7,012, or 72%, disclose an AI agent, and Claude accounts for 6,819 of the disclosures.

So the headline isn't the stars. It's that a free suite of creative and office apps is being built mostly by agents, in the open, and saying so.

Two honest notes. The ArtCraft site lists 13 apps and says free, no subscription, but makes no parity claim and no clean-room claim. Those words come from the coverage. And I haven't run any of them, so I can't tell you how close they get to the originals.
