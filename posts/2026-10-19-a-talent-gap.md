## Post body (copy from here)

A prompting gap is not why enterprises struggle to get AI into production.

Anthropic is putting $100 million into the Claude Frontier Academy, with a goal of 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. Look at what it actually teaches. A simulated enterprise deployment, from choosing a use case through security review to handover. Then a 12-week residency leading a real Claude use case inside your own organization. Then an assessment before you get the badge.

That's a deployment curriculum. The description is about shipping, not prompting.

Two things I'd keep in mind. Entry is by nomination from organizations, and the first cohort is consulting firms and large enterprises: Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and others. And the first full credentials aren't expected until early 2027, so nobody knows yet what the badge is worth.

Still, when the vendor tells you where the talent shortage is, it's worth hearing. It's the part after the demo.
