## Post body (copy from here)

Haiku 4.5 vs Haiku 5.5. $1.00 vs $0.10 per million input tokens. The cheap one is also the better one.

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7. Input is $0.10 per million tokens and output is $0.50, for requests up to 100k tokens. Haiku 4.5 was $1.00 and $5.00. Past 100k tokens it's $0.50 and $2.50, which Anthropic puts at roughly half the old price. Averaged out, about 75% cheaper.

On their own benchmark table, Haiku 5.5 scores 72.4% on the OSWorld 2.1 offline subset, up from 15.7%, and beats GPT-6 Luna on every benchmark where both have scores.

That's the vendor's table, so check it against your own tasks. But if your build-versus-buy math or your model routing was done more than a month ago, it's stale.

Same window: Sonnet 5.5 on September 28, described as up to 30% cheaper for most work, and Opus 5.5 on September 22, described as 40% cheaper than Opus 5.
