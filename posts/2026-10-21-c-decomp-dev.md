## Post body (copy from here)

AI is not why decomp.dev closed. The notice says so itself.

decomp.dev tracked progress on community game decompilation projects, where the goal is code that matches the original. Last Thursday its developer, a self-described sole hobbyist, disabled the main index and new project submissions. The headlines blamed AI. 404 Media's title says AI has made reverse engineering too easy.

The closing notice is more specific. It names hostile forks, where someone finishes another team's project with AI without contributing back. It names rushed ports built on decompilations the porter didn't help create. It says "decomp" had come to mean "PC port". And it says the progress tracking had become "a hollow numbers game."

Then the line that matters: "AI is not the issue by itself." Enforcing quality across hundreds of projects was more than one hobbyist could do.

That's a pattern I see at work too. Publish a progress number and it becomes a leaderboard. Agents just make the race faster.
